Frequently Asked Questions

As a client, simply log in to your dashboard, click "Book New Job", and follow the guided steps. You'll specify details like project type, rooms, preferred dates, and paint preferences. Our system will then match you with available painters.

Painters are assigned based on their service area, availability, skills, and client preferences. Admins can also manually assign painters to ensure the best fit.

We accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and PayPal for all painting jobs. Payments are securely processed through our platform.

You can update your personal and contact information by navigating to the 'Profile' section in your respective dashboard (Client Profile, Painter Profile, or Admin Settings).

Job cancellations can be managed through your dashboard. Please refer to our Terms of Service for cancellation policies and potential fees.
